Who he is

Born in North Carolina in 1930 and raised in Harlem, he left school at 17, served in the Marine Corps, and went on to a Harvard degree in economics and a doctorate from the University of Chicago. He taught at several universities, then spent decades as a senior fellow at the Hoover Institution.
