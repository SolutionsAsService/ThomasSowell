Corrections

Found a mistake? Add a source?

This is a fan site and it will have errors. The most useful thing you can send is a citation.

What helps most

  • A quotation: the book, edition and page, or a dated column or interview with a link.
  • A misattribution: where the saying really comes from.
  • A fact or a date: the source that shows the right one.
  • A photograph: its source and licence, or a request to remove it.

Open an issue on GitHub

There is no form on this page because there is nowhere for it to post. GitHub issues are public and answered in the open.

How a quote moves up

  1. Any quote becomes Book named only when a book, edition and page, or a dated original, shows the words. At present none has been checked.
  2. Widely attributed means popular, not checked. We never promote a quote because it is popular.
  3. Credited to someone else stays until a source in his own words is shown.

A fan-made appreciation site. It is not affiliated with Thomas Sowell or the Hoover Institution. Every quotation is marked with how well its source is known; if one is wrong, tell us.
