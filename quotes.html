Quotes

20 sayings attributed to him

Each carries a mark for how well its source is known. None has yet been checked against a printed page, so treat every mark as a lead, not a licence to quote. Listed best-sourced first.

The four marks

  • Book named, unchecked (2)A book is named, but its edition and page have not been checked. Say “attributed to him in” that book.
  • Widely attributed (8)Printed as his in many places; no source has been checked. Say “attributed to him”, not “he said”.
  • No source known (8)No source known to us and none searched for yet. Do not quote it as his.
  • Credited to someone else (2)On record as someone else's saying. Do not attribute it to him.
