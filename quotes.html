Quotes

20 sayings, and how far to trust each

Famous people collect sayings they never said. Each one here carries a mark for how well its source is known, so you can quote him without quoting someone else.

How to read the marks

  • Source namedA book is named. Edition and page are still to be checked.
  • Widely attributedPrinted everywhere as his; we have not yet checked the primary source.
  • DoubtfulWe could not place it. Treat it as unverified.
  • Probably not hisCredited to someone else, or not found in his writing.
